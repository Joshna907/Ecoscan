# Material-factor technical review — 2 November 2026

This is a source/compatibility review for EcoScan's screening model, not independent LCA certification. No new elastane, leather, rubber, polyurethane-foam or unspecified-material factor was approved for the bundled fibre-production dataset.

| Candidate | Finding | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Van der Velden et al., LCA benchmarking study](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11367-013-0626-9) | Models elastane base material using flexible polyurethane foam and includes distinct spinning/texturing/weaving stages. Polymer/fabric results cannot be relabelled as a representative elastane fibre factor. | Do not silently substitute its proxy into the existing fibre model. |
| [Ecobalyse public process data](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/cout-environnemental-des-procedes-textile-alimentaire-et-objet) | Published environmental-cost process scores use weighting/normalisation. They are not kg CO2e; detailed climate inventories require appropriate data access. | No environmental-score-to-carbon conversion. |
| [LWG 2024 summary](https://www.leatherworkinggroup.com/fileadmin/user_upload/LWG_LCA_2024_Summary_.pdf) | Reports 22.48 kg CO2e per square metre of finished leather, spanning upstream and tanning/finishing. No compatible per-kg fibre factor is supplied in the public summary. | Do not assume an areal mass or place finished leather inside the fibre boundary. Obtain the relevant licensed inventory and product specification. |
| [Idemat data/download terms](https://www.ecocostsvalue.com/data-tools-books/) and [publisher homepage](https://www.ecocostsvalue.com/) | Publisher distinguishes access to spreadsheet results from software use; its homepage excludes software use from the stated open access. Material records also distinguish pellets, textile processes and finished materials. | Do not bundle spreadsheet factors without software-use permission and checking the actual material/process record. |
| [LYCRA/QIRA screening LCA white paper](https://www.myqira.com/fileadmin/downloads/06_LYCRA_fiber_made_with_QIRA/QIRA_LYCRA_White_Paper.pdf) | Publishes a relative reduction for a representative manufacturing route, not a compatible absolute generic elastane factor for this model. | A percentage improvement is not an absolute factor. |

The seven existing sourced fibre factors remain unchanged. The model now reports the known contribution of supported fractions without renormalising them to 100%. For a measured 200 g 98% cotton/2% elastane garment, 196 g of cotton is calculated and 4 g elastane remains unknown. Missing shell/lining/padding/upper/sole mass is also listed separately. A low/high range on the known portion is not an uncertainty bound for the entire garment. The category benchmark remains separate and rewards remain unaffected. Methodology version is now `materials-v2`; older saved results retain their recorded version.

Imported snapshots must now include a review record identifying the reviewer, review time and common assessment method, and explicit confirmation of unit, boundary and software-use checks. This is an operator-controlled record, not an automatic guarantee that a scientific assertion or licence is correct. Source methods must match the reviewed method. Unspecified `other` and finished `leather` cannot be imported into the fibre-production model. A finished-material model needs its own boundary and applicable units rather than bypassing this rule.

To finish data coverage: obtain a software-licensed, absolute elastane fibre inventory with its production route, geography, assessment method and source uncertainty; select compatible factors for the remaining fibres; and build a separately bounded finished-material model for leather/soles/foams. Current code can load an approved fibre snapshot, but this review did not obtain those external data rights or approve a new elastane value. Values in unit-test fixtures are deliberately artificial and must not be used as product data.
